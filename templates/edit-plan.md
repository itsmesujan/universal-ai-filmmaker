## Rhythm

- Shot lengths varied deliberately: state where the cut tightens and why.
- Default to cuts; use dissolves/fades only for time or consciousness changes.
- `l_cut`/`j_cut` to smooth dialogue scenes; `hard_cut_on_action` and `match_cut` for energy.

## Trim rules

Cut to the emotional beat, not to the generated clip length. If a clip's exit state
contradicts the next shot's entry state, insert a neutral shot rather than stretching time.
