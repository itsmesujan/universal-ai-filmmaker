# Continuity and Identity

The continuity ledger is authoritative for recurring assets.

## Character continuity

Track face, hair, body proportions, costume, accessories, palette, age presentation, voice, and movement style.

## World continuity

Track location layout, entrances/exits, landmarks, props, weather, time, lighting direction, and environmental damage.

## Screen geography

Track eyelines, screen direction, camera side of the action, subject position, and spatial relationships.

## Repair order

When a shot fails, fix the smallest layer that explains the failure:

1. reference asset
2. keyframe
3. shot design
4. model adapter/prompt
5. model/provider

Do not change the whole project to repair one shot.
